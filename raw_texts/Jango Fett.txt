Jango Fett was a famed Mandalorian human male bounty hunter and the clone template of the Grand Army of the Republic.
Known as the best bounty hunter in the galaxy during the final years of the Galactic Republic, Fett was proficient in marksmanship as well as unarmed combat.
The Mandalorian armor that he wore featured various weapons and gadgets, including a flamethrower, dual WESTAR-34 blaster pistols, and a jetpack.
His personal starship was the Firespray-31-class patrol and attack craft Slave I.
